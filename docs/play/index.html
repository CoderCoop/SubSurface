Subsurface

You are a subterranean pathfinder. Cut a path for the fluid and get it down to the crystal.

  1. Drag anywhere to dig. Your finger carves the ground away. There is no undo — a cut is permanent.
  2. Get 85% of the fluid into the collector at the bottom. Beat it by more for a higher grade: 85% ★92% ★★97% ★★★
  3. Miss it and the drains take the payload. They sit either side of the collector, and anything reaching them is gone for good.
  4. Clearing a level unlocks the next one. They get harder in order: clay first, then sand, then fractured rock — and the ground you can afford to miss by shrinks the whole way down.
Clay — cuts clean, holds fluid Sand — collapses, drinks fluid, gives it back under pressure Fractured rock — shatters into chunks that can plug a passage Bedrock — cannot be cut

To install: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
